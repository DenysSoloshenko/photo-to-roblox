# Springer Park open-data study — sources and reuse

Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
Attribution: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

`source.json` and the derived geometry database `scene.json` are distributed
under ODbL 1.0. Source data was retrieved from the official OSM API on
2026-09-20, selecting ways in a small area around Springer Park, Burnaby.
The local projection and selected tags are recorded in `source.json`.
Both databases are freely available alongside the case; no account is required.

The generator is `scripts/build_neighbourhood_case.py` in the project repository:
https://github.com/DenysSoloshenko/photo-to-roblox
It creates procedural facades and planting without photographs or map tiles.
The SVG plan is rendered from the vector database. Rendered images are shown
with adjacent OSM attribution on the case page; preserve this notice when
redistributing them. The video includes attribution in every scene.

The Roblox file is an editable, approximate study. Keep this notice and the
attribution when sharing it. It is not a surveyed digital twin; building
details and heights are simplified, missing floor counts are estimated, and
planting is decorative. This is a separate rebuild, not a relicensing of the
earlier local Google-based research prototype. No Google imagery, Street View
screenshots, customer photos or real business logos are included.

Roblox Studio Play, walkability, mobile performance and release QA still need
testing. This demonstration does not represent a live AI result, a delivered
customer order or an offer to build an entire district for the standard fee.
